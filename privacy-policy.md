# Republic Legions Discord Bot

## Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 27, 2026

### 1. Introduction

The Republic Legions Discord Bot ("the Bot") respects user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and how it is protected.

### 2. Information We Collect

Depending on which features are enabled, the Bot may collect or process:

- Discord User IDs.
- Discord Server (Guild) IDs.
- Discord usernames and display names when needed for bot functionality.
- Member activity timestamps for inactivity tracking.
- Bot command interactions and configuration settings.
- Technical information necessary for monitoring server availability.

**Message Content:** The Bot is not intended to collect or store the contents of members' ordinary Discord messages for inactivity tracking. Inactivity tracking uses activity timestamps rather than retaining message contents.

### 3. How Information Is Used

Information processed by the Bot may be used to:

- Provide Discord commands and functionality.
- Monitor ARMA 3 server availability.
- Monitor TeamSpeak server availability.
- Track member inactivity when that feature is enabled.
- Support administrative and moderation functions.
- Troubleshoot errors and maintain reliable operation.
- Protect the Bot against misuse and unauthorized access.

### 4. Data Storage and Security

Information necessary for the Bot's operation may be stored on its hosting environment.

Reasonable measures are taken to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

### 5. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Bot's functionality.

Activity records and other operational information may be deleted when no longer needed, when the relevant feature is disabled, or when deletion is requested and appropriate.

### 6. Information Sharing

Republic Legions does not sell or rent users' personal information.

Information may be processed through Discord and other third-party services necessary for the Bot's functionality.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect the security and operation of the Bot.

### 7. Third-Party Services

The Bot interacts with external services, including:

- Discord
- ARMA 3 game servers
- TeamSpeak servers
- Bot hosting providers

These services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

Republic Legions does not control the privacy practices of these external providers.

### 8. User Data Requests

Users may request information about data associated with their Discord account.

Requests to access, correct, or delete applicable stored information can be submitted to the Republic Legions Administration.

### 9. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children under 13. Users must comply with Discord's applicable age requirements and terms.

### 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the Bot's features and data practices change.

Updates will be published at the official Privacy Policy URL.
